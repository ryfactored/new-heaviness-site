Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

New Heaviness is a personal tool run by its owner to maintain YouTube playlists of new heavy music videos. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

  • Public YouTube information (video titles, channels, publish dates, view counts) through the YouTube Data API.
  • The owner's own New Heaviness YouTube channel, only to create and update its playlists. Access is granted by the owner through Google sign-in with the YouTube scope.

What it stores

A list of the videos on the playlist and weekly notes about which videos were found, kept privately by the owner. No personal information about viewers or other people is collected, stored, sold or shared.

This website

The home page shows the playlist in an embedded YouTube player, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and video thumbnails loaded from YouTube. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. When you play a video, YouTube may store information on your device under the Google Privacy Policy.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ryfactored/newheaviness.
